Key facts (rule numbers from Core Rules 2026-10-09):
- Deck (103): 1 Champion Legend (sets Domain Identity) · Main Deck ≥40 incl. Chosen Champion (champion unit matching legend tag) · max 3 copies per name · max 3 Signature cards total · Rune Deck exactly 12 (legend's domains) · Battlefields per mode (Duel: bring 3, use 1).
- Setup (110–118): legend + chosen champion out, shuffle decks, random first player, draw 4, mulligan: set aside up to 2, draw that many, recycle the set-aside.
- Duel (487): victory 8; 2 battlefields in play; player going second channels 1 extra rune on first Channel Phase.
- Turn (315–317): Awaken (ready all) → Beginning (start effects, then Hold scoring) → Channel 2 runes → Draw 1 (empty deck = Burn Out) → Main (rune pool empties at start) → Ending (heal all units, "this turn" ends, pool empties).
- Runes (160–168): basic rune: exhaust → add 1 Energy; recycle → add 1 Power of its domain. Energy = number cost, Power = domain symbols. Pool empties at start of Main Phase and end of turn.
- Units (140–146): enter exhausted (Accelerate pays 1 energy + 1 power to enter ready); played to base or a battlefield you control; Standard Move = exhaust, base↔battlefield (Ganking: battlefield→battlefield); not in showdown/combat or closed state. Damage ≥ Might kills; heal at end of turn and after combat.
- Gear (147–152): enters ready, played to base; unattached gear at a battlefield is recalled.
- Spells (153–159): own turn, open neutral state; Action = also during showdowns; Reaction = also on a chain (resolves first).
- Chain (327–340): LIFO; units/gear/add-resource abilities resolve immediately after finalizing (can't be responded to).
- Showdown (341–348): starts when a battlefield becomes contested; contester gets Focus; players alternate playing Action/Reaction or pass; all pass → ends.
- Combat (461–468): attacker = who contested. Sum Might each side, assign lethal damage one unit at a time (Tank first, Backline last), dealt simultaneously; kill; heal all; attackers recalled if defenders survive; sole survivor establishes control → Conquer.
- Scoring (469–474): Conquer (gain control) or Hold (control at your Beginning Phase); once per battlefield per turn; final point via Conquer only if you scored every battlefield this turn, else draw 1 instead (Hold has no such limit). Win checked in cleanup: ≥ victory score and more than everyone.
- Burn Out (432): recycle trash into deck, an opponent of your choice gains 1 point, then finish the draw.
- Buff (701): +1 Might, max one per unit. Mighty = Might ≥ 5. Stunned unit deals no combat damage this turn.

Marked uncertain / simplified in the app:
- No max hand size: stated in the quick-start PDF; no hand limit found in Core Rules.
- Full priority/cleanup procedure is simplified for teaching; the app links to the Core Rules for edge cases.
